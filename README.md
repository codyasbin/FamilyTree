# Codyasbin

### Every name is a story. Every connection is a part of you.

Families are more than names arranged across generations. They are the people whose choices, courage, traditions, struggles, and love quietly shaped the lives that came after them.

Codyasbin is a private family-tree studio created to help you remember those connections. It gives families a simple place to record relatives, preserve personal stories, understand relationships, and keep memories from disappearing with time.

## Why knowing your roots matters

Knowing where we come from can help us understand who we are.

Our relatives carry pieces of our shared history: the places our families once called home, the work they did, the challenges they survived, the customs they protected, and the values they passed forward. Learning about them can turn distant names into real people—and scattered memories into a story that belongs to the whole family.

A family tree can help us:

- Preserve stories before they are forgotten.
- Understand how relatives and family branches are connected.
- Give younger generations a stronger sense of identity and belonging.
- Remember loved ones through their names, photographs, places, and life details.
- Discover patterns, traditions, and journeys shared across generations.
- Create meaningful conversations between children, parents, grandparents, and extended family.

You do not need a famous ancestor or a perfectly documented history. A remembered nickname, an old photograph, a birthplace, or a small family story is already worth preserving.

## Built for living family stories

Codyasbin lets you:

- Build and arrange an interactive family tree.
- Add parents, children, siblings, and partners.
- Record birth years, locations, biographies, notes, and sources.
- Upload portraits and open them in a full-size viewer.
- Organize relatives into clans and family groups.
- Create, rename, and delete groups, and move people between family branches.
- Browse everyone in a directory or chronological timeline.
- Search, zoom, pan, and automatically arrange the tree.
- Import or export the complete family tree as a JSON file.
- Undo and redo changes while editing.

## Your family history stays with you

Codyasbin is local-first. Your family tree is stored in your browser on your device; it is not automatically uploaded to a server.

Use **Export tree** regularly to create a backup. The exported file can be saved somewhere safe or shared with trusted relatives. Use **Import** to restore that file later.

> Family history can contain sensitive information. Ask permission before adding private stories or photographs of living relatives, and share exported files thoughtfully.

## A tree grows one conversation at a time

Start with what you know: yourself, your parents, and your grandparents. Then speak with relatives and ask open questions:

- What was your childhood home like?
- Which family traditions do you remember?
- What stories were told about your parents or grandparents?
- Where did the family live before coming here?
- Which moments changed the direction of the family?
- What would you want future generations to remember?

Dates are useful, but stories give a family tree its soul.

## Run Codyasbin

No installation or build process is required. Open `index.html` in a modern browser.

For the best local experience, you can also serve the folder with any simple static web server, for example:

```bash
python -m http.server 8000
```

Then visit `http://localhost:8000`.
